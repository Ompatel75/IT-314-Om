# IT314 – Software Engineering
## Requirement Elicitation Techniques
**Project:** AI Software Project Health & Risk Analyzer

---

### 1.1 Individual Developers

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Observation

**Reason:** Developers are directly involved in coding, commits, pull requests, and issue handling. Interviews can help us understand their problems, workload, and expectations from the analyzer. Observation can help us understand how they actually work and where delays or bottlenecks occur.

---

### 1.2 Engineering / Team Leads + Senior Code Reviewers

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Observation

**Reason:** Team leads and senior reviewers are responsible for monitoring team progress, workload, and code reviews. Interviews can help us understand what information they use while making decisions. Observation can show how they monitor review queues and team-level problems.

---

### 1.3 Project Manager

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Questionnaire

**Reason:** The Project Manager needs an overall view of the project's progress, schedule, delivery, and risks. An interview helps us understand their requirements in detail, while a questionnaire can collect structured information about the reports and indicators they consider important.

---

### 1.4 Scrum Master / Agile Coach

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Brainstorming

**Reason:** The project follows the Agile/SCRUM methodology, so the Scrum Master can explain how sprint progress, blockers, unfinished work, and retrospectives should be represented in the system. Brainstorming can help the team identify useful Agile-related indicators.

---

### 1.5 Product Owner / Product Manager

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Questionnaire

**Reason:** The Product Owner connects the technical progress of the project with product scope and business priorities. Interviews can help us understand roadmap and backlog requirements, while questionnaires can help collect structured information about priorities and changes.

---

### 1.6 QA / Test Lead

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Observation

**Reason:** The QA/Test Lead provides the quality perspective of the project. Interviews can help us understand testing requirements and common quality problems. Observation can help us understand how defects are tracked, tested, and resolved in practice.

---

### 1.7 DevOps / System Admin + Security / InfoSec

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Document Analysis

**Reason:** This group is responsible for deployment, system access, security, authentication, credentials, and integrations. Interviews help us understand operational and security requirements, while existing technical and security documentation provides formal constraints.

---

### 1.8 AI/ML Engineers

**Elicitation Technique:** Interview + Document Analysis

**Reason:** AI/ML engineers are responsible for the AI/GenAI analysis layer of the system. Interviews help us understand how the AI should behave, while documentation provides information about models, APIs, limitations, and technical constraints.
